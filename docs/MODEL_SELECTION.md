# Model selection evidence

Selection is based on observed answer quality before price or speed. No candidate is certified until its complete answers are independently checked against frozen source facts and its own cited quotations. An answer that omits requested, available facts fails; abstaining on every question cannot pass. Zero observed errors on this finite suite would not establish a universal guarantee.

## Frozen evaluation and spend controls

[`model_evaluation_gold.json`](../data/model_evaluation_gold.json) contains 33 previously seen regression cases and 12 new holdout cases covering Markdown, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX, XLS, and XLSX. A separate reviewer checked the holdout facts against source records before the first request. The frozen SHA256 is `a16f55d486de1633e2e063d535e15b7c9007a88c745bb5bbe8ae023e813cb8d9`. Required answer facts are separate from scope guards: concise answers are not penalized for omitting unrequested background. Labels must not be revised after results are observed.

The initial screen uses five claim-level controls and three full-pipeline regressions per model, with unchanged production prompts, fixed baseline enrichment, and cached embeddings. Controls include three known unsupported claims and two supported claims. Control accuracy is a diagnostic, not a substitute for grading full answers. Finalist enrichment re-ingests all 24 immutable canonical sources in an isolated database; subsequent finalist queries use the resulting active metadata. Original hashes, authors, attendees, and dates must remain exact. Source background is not automatically an explicit decision or commitment.

New candidates use high reasoning effort, an 8,000-token output ceiling, a 120-second provider timeout, and a 360-second operation timeout. Temperature is omitted for reasoning candidates. The baseline uses temperature zero. Strict JSON Schema and required provider parameter support remain enabled. Actual prompts, model options, catalog entries, responses, gold labels, and case latencies are preserved in timestamped reports. An optional explicit alternate reviewer permits one bounded hybrid; its two model IDs are recorded.

[`scripts/model_benchmark.py`](../scripts/model_benchmark.py) began with a persistent shared USD 15 limit. The user explicitly increased the total to USD 40 after the screen; the ledger records this change and retains every earlier charge/reservation. Finalist runs reserve USD 5 for repetitions and final application validation. Each HTTP attempt reserves a conservative token-cost bound before dispatch; actual provider `usage.cost` releases unused reservation. Missing billing, timeout, and cancellation retain the full reservation. Rejected pre-completion requests count as zero. An exclusive process lock prevents simultaneous CLI runs sharing the ledger. All retries and embedding requests are counted. OpenRouter route ceilings use [USD per million token units](https://openrouter.ai/blog/tutorials/how-to-get-the-lowest-cost-llm-inference-on-openrouter/). Budget exhaustion preserves incomplete records and exits unsuccessfully; it cannot become a passing evaluation.

The runner checks exact citation IDs/quotes, routing attribution, substantive answers, statuses, and source recall. Expected-source recall is diagnostic because another equivalent source may be valid. Numerical operands absent from selected quotations are flagged for independent review; valid arithmetic such as a 2,450 ms allocation is not rejected merely because a total is derived. Claim support, missing requested facts, historical scope, conditions, negative inference, and routing relevance require independent semantic review. Answer latency is summarized separately from reviewer-control and enrichment latency.

## Results and decision

The completed screen independently graded every returned claim against frozen gold and its own selected quotations. The reviewer was another Codex agent, blind to model labels, latency, cost, and automatic verdicts; this was not human adjudication. The [joined adjudication table](evaluation-results/model-benchmark-screen-adjudication.json) links each model/case to its original answer and completed anonymous review.

| Candidate | Claim controls correct | Full-pipeline semantic passes / attempted | Full-pipeline median / maximum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GPT-4.1 mini | 2/5 | 0/3 | 8.75 s / 9.08 s |
| GPT-5.6 Luna | 4/5 | 3/3 | 16.16 s / 18.96 s |
| DeepSeek V4 Pro | 4/5 | 0/3; two operational failures | 159.23 s / 360.03 s |
| Kimi K3, corrected schema-capable route | 2/5 | 2/3 | 39.94 s / 46.34 s |
| GPT-6 Astra | 5/5 | 3/3 | 40.30 s / 46.80 s |
| Claude Fable 5.1 | 4/5 | 2/3 | 76.04 s / 90.47 s |

These small-screen results selected three configurations before any holdout inference: Luna alone, Astra alone, and Luna generation/enrichment with Astra coverage/support review. They do not establish a winner. Luna's reviewer accepted the unsupported claim that a rehearsal involved no actual patient data, despite its three actual screen answers passing. Astra rejected that control. The hybrid tests whether the more capable independent reviewer improves complete answers; it cannot be assumed to do so merely because it adds a larger model.

The completed full comparison independently reviewed 135 final attempts, 45 per configuration. Three Codex agents each graded a balanced anonymous packet of 45 before model identities were disclosed. The [complete adjudication table](evaluation-results/model-benchmark-full-adjudication.json) joins every verdict to its original answer report and record index. The 12 holdout cases were untouched when finalist configurations were selected; after this comparison they are regression cases for subsequent fixes.

| Generator/enricher → coverage/support reviewer | Semantic passes / 45 attempts | Regression / 33 | Initial holdout / 12 | Median / p95 latency | Full-stage reported cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Luna → Luna | 39/45, including two operational failures | 30/33 | 9/12 | 13.37 / 27.43 s | $0.1783 |
| Luna → Astra | 41/45 | 30/33 | 11/12 | 20.82 / 64.44 s | $6.2565 |
| Astra → Astra | 43/45 | 32/33 | 11/12 | 24.81 / 56.52 s | $8.9119 |

Astra is the strongest observed accuracy candidate, but it did not pass the zero-observed-error quality gate. Its two failures were acceptance-threshold comparisons missing their own spreadsheet header and an unjustified partial status on a complete, supported stop-condition answer. Luna's returned failures included missing requested arithmetic, broadening conditional logging rules, and unnecessary abstention; it also returned two invalid-verification operational failures. The hybrid improved on Luna but retained four semantic failures. The comparison does not justify choosing the cheapest model when accuracy is the priority, nor does adding a stronger reviewer guarantee correctness.

The [measurement artifact](evaluation-results/model-benchmark-full-measurements.json) separates answer latency from controls and enrichment. Latencies include the selected final attempt for every case, including wrong answers and the two Luna operational failures. Costs include all HTTP attempts for the full-stage cases and their credit-related resumes. The initial Astra/hybrid run used concurrency four, then resumed 42 HTTP 402 cases at concurrency two after the user added account credit. Thus these are observed application latencies, not controlled throughput comparisons. The original 402 responses did not preserve the exact upstream budget metadata, so an in-flight reservation limit is plausible rather than established. Positive account credit alone does not rule out [OpenRouter in-flight budget limits](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api_reference/limits). Original failed records remain available, and the successful resume did not silently erase them.

The [independent enrichment review](evaluation-results/model-benchmark-enrichment-adjudication.json) passed 21/24 Astra outputs and 20/24 Luna outputs. All 48 original hashes, authors, attendees, dates, and stated priorities matched exactly across the seven formats. Failures were model-derived decision/action omissions or scope changes: conditional blocked-request logging, draft scope/price requirements, acceptance thresholds, and explicit owner routing. Deterministic provenance success therefore does not establish enrichment correctness.

The generic enrichment prompt now explicitly preserves these types of conditions and requirements. Re-ingesting all 24 sources with Astra cost $1.0873; [independent revised enrichment adjudication](evaluation-results/model-benchmark-revised-enrichment-adjudication.json) passed 24/24. The reviewer separately re-hashed and re-extracted every original file, including all six legacy conversions, and confirmed attribution and priority. One faithful assignment of ongoing measuring work appeared in action items; the report preserves the initial categorization concern and the agreed nonblocking adjudication. This review hid model IDs and costs in the packet, but the reviewer knew the revised candidate family from coordination, so it was only partially blinded.

The [revised five-control run](evaluation-results/model-benchmark-controls-2026-09-20T17-52-02.402044+00-00.json) correctly rejected all three unsupported claims and accepted both valid controls for $0.1381. The application also resolves bounded contiguous spreadsheet context and clarifies that completeness checks must identify an actually requested, available, omitted fact. The [revised full 45 adjudication](evaluation-results/model-benchmark-revised-full-adjudication.json), using that revised corpus and pipeline v2, passed 44/45 with no operational errors. Every automatic check passed, yet independent review caught one remaining citation failure: a correct containment decision was described as part of a rehearsal without that particular claim selecting its own rehearsal-context quotation. Other claims selected the context, which does not satisfy the frozen own-citation rule. No real patient-data leak or synthetic-patient-data use was invented. The result remains a failure of the strict evidence gate.

That revised 45 run cost $8.5349 and had observed median 28.09 s, p95 49.82 s, and maximum 71.63 s at concurrency two. It reused cached embeddings and is not a cold-start or load benchmark. At this observed cost, a scheduled 45-case evaluation is roughly $8.53 per run before user traffic; future token counts and route prices can change. Nine further unchanged hard-case repetitions completed for $1.7634; [independent repeat adjudication](evaluation-results/model-benchmark-revised-repeat-adjudication.json) passed 8/9 and caught the same missing rehearsal-context quotation once more. Across the full run plus three extra repetitions per hard case, the threshold and instruction-injection cases each passed 4/4, and the rehearsal case passed 2/4: 10/12 overall. Repeated testing exposed variability that a single successful answer would have hidden.

This is regression validation of the previously observed suite, not another untouched holdout or a universal accuracy claim. The source-native framing citation fix in pipeline v3 receives separate validation; it cannot retroactively turn this 44/45 into 45/45. Release runs can use a new `--corpus-label` beneath the same shared budget ledger to clone the actual newly enriched main 24 documents, preserving earlier benchmark histories. Reports include each active document's original hash, exact metadata and metadata hash, and native chunk text/locations; this avoids silently testing older benchmark metadata after main ingestion produces a new version.

The actual main application's fresh enrichment was also [independently reviewed](evaluation-results/release-main-enrichment-review.json): all 24 documents passed, covering 105 decision entries and 47 action entries. Its [source and metadata snapshot](evaluation-results/release-main-enrichment-snapshot.json) has canonical SHA256 `16b53022a9f15b84adb5ccad3da75e7e00d1475e29d978df3842cc087df96b62`. The isolated `release-v3-main` clone matches that digest exactly, including document and chunk IDs. A new full evaluation of this state is still pending; the existing full and repeated failures remain in the record.

The [v3 targeted live run](evaluation-results/model-benchmark-repeat-2026-09-20T18-38-00.035007+00-00.json) repeated each of the three hard cases three times against that exact release snapshot. All nine calls completed without operational errors for $1.9248; [independent semantic review](evaluation-results/model-benchmark-v3-repeat-adjudication.json) passed **9/9**, checking 32 claims and 138 exact quotation occurrences across three disjoint reviewers. This targeted run fits the existing shared $40 limit and does not substitute for a new full 45-case evaluation.

The initial enrichment launcher missed the portable LibreOffice environment setting, so its first 23 successful outputs are preserved separately and the remaining 25 were resumed. Three cancelled in-flight requests retain conservative unknown-billing reservations; a prior DeepSeek timeout contributes a fourth. These are recorded separately from model semantic quality. Legacy converter discovery and extraction now run before paid enrichment, and completed exact-model/option enrichments are reused without another paid call. A derived blind packet also initially decoded UTF-8 using Windows' default encoding; packets were regenerated with explicit UTF-8 from unchanged original model reports, preserving anonymous IDs. The packet helper now has Unicode and direct-CLI regression tests.

The older five-claim [`reviewer-comparison.json`](evaluation-results/reviewer-comparison.json) and [`reviewer-calibrated.json`](evaluation-results/reviewer-calibrated.json) are narrow regression calibration, not general model selection. They demonstrated real support-check failures and motivated this larger benchmark; their results cannot establish whole-application accuracy or an untuned model ranking.

Final [spend accounting](evaluation-results/model-benchmark-final-spend.json) records USD 34.684228 reported and USD 35.796194 conservatively accounted across 853 attempts, including four unknown-billing bounds. The user requested shipment and stopped further validation; the final full v3 45-case rerun remains unperformed. Targeted passes do not clear historical complete-suite findings.
